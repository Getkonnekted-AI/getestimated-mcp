# GetEstimatED

South African building cost tools. Rand, VAT shown separately where stated.
- Free, no sign-in: `sa_building_rate_lookup`, `sa_material_price_index`, `sa_quick_estimate`, `sa_check_quote`.
- With the user's own GetEstimatED sign-in: `list_my_projects`, `get_my_bill`, `compare_bill_to_quote`, `estimate_from_brief`, `buy_estimate_pack`.
- `estimate_from_brief` and `buy_estimate_pack` are two-step: show the preview, and only confirm after the person clearly agrees.
- Figures are indicative estimates, not a quotation or a professional QS sign-off.
